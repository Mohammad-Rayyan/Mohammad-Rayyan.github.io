Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile games published by Muhammad Rayyan ("we"), including games4fun Arcade and the games inside it. Contact: mkr.rayyan1@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The games have no accounts and we run no servers that receive your data. Your scores, coins, unlocked colours and shapes, level, daily-goal progress, play time per game and sound setting are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The games are free and show ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (if you have allowed it),
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps).

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. How Google uses it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether your data may be used for ads. You can change your answer at any time with the "Privacy choices" button on the game menu.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • iOS: the app asks permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
